Email signature

Fill in your details, copy, and paste into Gmail. Images load from the Breakout Learning site, so the signature stays up to date on its own.

  1. Fill in your name and title.
  2. Click Copy signature.
  3. In Gmail, open Settings → See all settings → General → Signature.
  4. Delete the old signature content and paste.
  5. Scroll down and click Save changes.
